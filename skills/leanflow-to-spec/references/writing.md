# Write for the reader

Read when drafting or revising an English or Indonesian human-facing plan, specification, design, or substantial report. Apply the matching language section; for mixed-language text, assess each passage in its own language. Reading this reference does not select specification capture or authorize rewriting supplied material. Routine short answers need no separate editorial pass.

## Shared checks

Establish the audience, purpose, language, register, and required format from the request and source. Lead with the decision, finding, or requested action. Use concrete verbs and traceable claims; remove empty framing and repetition, not necessary reasoning or qualifications. Leave effective wording unchanged.

Preserve requirements, rationale, evidence, citations, numbers, units, uncertainty, and the author's meaning. Keep code, commands, paths, APIs, identifiers, and precise domain terms exact unless their change is authorized. Do not invent measurements, references, actors, or personal experience to make prose more convincing. An audit reports issues; it does not authorize a rewrite.

AI-facing instructions and structured output need precise obligations, consistent terminology, and valid schemas, not stylistic variation. Never infer authorship or promise detector evasion. Vocabulary is a diagnostic cue, not a blacklist or automatic synonym rule.

## English

- Prefer a direct action over vague business language: name what changes and why. Inspect words such as “leverage,” “enhance,” or “seamless” for missing substance; retain established technical meanings such as “test harness.”
- Use connectors only when they express a real relationship. Remove repetitive “furthermore” or “it is important to note” framing without removing the warning or qualification.
- Preserve distinctions between evidence, estimates, and conclusions. “Not statistically significant” must not become “unimportant”; do not replace unsupported praise with a different unsupported claim.
- Match the reader's register. Plain language does not require slang, forced contractions, or arbitrary sentence-length variation.

## Bahasa Indonesia

- Sesuaikan ragam bahasa dengan pembaca dan tujuan. Tulisan resmi tetap boleh formal; jangan memaksakan bahasa percakapan, slang, atau susunan kalimat hasil terjemahan harfiah bahasa Inggris.
- Utamakan tindakan dan objek yang jelas. Periksa klaim seperti “meningkatkan” atau “mengoptimalkan”: jelaskan aspek dan pembandingnya jika tersedia, tanpa mengarang angka. Pertahankan istilah yang sudah tepat, termasuk “mencakup,” “interaksi,” dan “berdasarkan.”
- Gunakan penghubung seperti “selain itu” atau “dengan demikian” jika hubungan antargagasan memang mendukungnya. Hapus pengantar yang mubazir, bukan hubungan penalarannya.
- Pilih bentuk aktif atau pasif sesuai fokus informasi. Pertahankan bentuk pasif saat prosedur, objek, atau hasil menjadi fokus; jangan menambahkan pelaku yang tidak disebutkan sumber.
- Jaga batas bukti: “dapat,” “diperkirakan,” dan “belum dapat disimpulkan” bukan kelemahan yang harus dihapus. “Tidak signifikan secara statistik” tidak sama dengan “tidak penting”; hubungan tidak otomatis berarti sebab akibat.

## Before delivery

Compare the result with its source for changed meaning, certainty, terminology, technical details, and required structure. Keep unresolved questions visible. Report unsupported claims rather than manufacturing support.
